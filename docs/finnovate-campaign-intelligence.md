## 1. Product Overview: ₹999 Financial Health Checkup (FHC)

Finnovate's ₹999 Financial Health Checkup is a comprehensive, fee-only personal finance assessment delivered by SEBI-registered investment advisors.

- **Offer Price**: ₹999 one-time fee (statutory flat fee, zero hidden commissions).
- **Core Value Proposition**: Unbiased portfolio audit, asset allocation health check, insurance adequacy assessment, and customized tax-saving roadmap.
- **Target Audience**: Salaried professionals (ages 28–45) and family wealth builders in Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities in India.
- **Regulatory Framework**: Fully compliant with SEBI Investment Advisers Regulations (2013). Strict prohibition of guaranteed return claims or speculative advice.

Analysis of top-converting cohorts reveals three distinct audience segments driving 82% of all checkup completions:

### Segment A: "The Anxious Tech Professional" (Top Converting)
- **Demographics**: Ages 28–38, IT/Tech/SaaS professionals, annual household income ₹18L–₹45L.
- **Top Channels**: Meta Retargeting (Instagram Reels & Stories) + WhatsApp Direct.
- **Conversion CAC**: ₹320–₹345 (38% below account average).

## 5. SEBI Regulatory Compliance Rulebook

All copy variants and marketing communications must strictly adhere to the following mandatory guidelines:

- **Prohibited Words**: "Guaranteed returns", "100% risk-free", "Double your money", "Assured profit", "Multibagger tips".
- **Mandatory Disclosures**: Include statutory advisory disclaimer: *"Investment in securities markets are subject to market risks. Read all related documents carefully before investing."*
- **Transparent Pricing**: Explicitly display the ₹999 fee without hidden charges.
